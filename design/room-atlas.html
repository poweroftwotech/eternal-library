Library Room Atlas

Twenty room types for the Library, in two batches, built from one shared kit of modules on a 4 m grid. Each plate shows a mock of the room, which parts are hand-placed in the Godot editor and which are picked or laid out at generation time, the modules it needs, and a prompt for producing a painted concept.

The mocks are drawn live in this page at the game's texel scale. Reroll sockets re-seeds the variation points, so each click shows another version of the same room.

One kit, three ways to build

Every room uses the same modules. What changes is who decides where they go: you in the editor, the generator, or both. The mode is a property of the room template, not of the modules.

Authored

Landmarks and key rooms. You place every module by hand. A few sockets swap dressing (plants, lamp state, damage) so return visits show time passing.

Godot: a normal .tscn built from module scenes with grid snapping. Sockets are a small VariantSocket node.

Hybrid

Settlement hubs and recurring room types. You build the shell and mark sockets; the generator fills each socket from a weighted list filtered by stratum, faction and age.

Godot: VariantSocket exports an array of candidate scenes with weights and tags; it previews candidate 0 in the editor and picks by seed at load.

Generated

Connective tissue and large repetitive spaces. A layout grammar fills a cell grid, then picks a module for each cell edge (wall, opening, connector). Layouts stay simple on purpose.

Godot: modules carry Marker3D connectors (conn_*). The area seed is saved, so a revisited area rebuilds the same, then ages.

The twenty room types

Nine of the twenty are fully generated, seven are hybrid and four are built by hand. The generated layouts reduce to corridors, grids, lanes, rings, terraces or spans, which a grammar can produce without looking random. The authored and hybrid rooms are the ones players will navigate by. Most of the second batch reuses a first-batch generator: the cistern is R03's grid with water, the ossuary is R02's corridor at half scale, and the yard shares R05's courtyard.

Candidates for a third batch

Each of these reuses most of the kit above and adds one or two modules.

  • Audience Hall (Talar)A faction's seat of power: columned porch, throne iwan, petitioners. Authored, one per faction.
  • Map ArchiveRooms of route maps and survey tables; where the player buys and corrects maps. Hybrid.
  • Wind-tower Hall (Badgir)Air shafts that move cold air and sound; airflow as a navigation and stealth rule. Hybrid.
  • Kitchens and GranarySettlement food stores and big hearths; cooking techniques taught here. Generated from R05 cells.
  • Lamp ObservatoryA domed hall of suspended lamps mapping something nobody agrees on. Authored landmark.
  • Creature WarrenShelves and stacks taken over by nesting creatures; ecology as a room. Generated overlay on R02 and R03.
  • Prison CellsFaction cells and an oubliette; capture, escape and possession of guards. Generated from R17 niches at room scale.
  • Collapsed StacksA ruin pass that can age any generated room: fallen vaults, rubble ramps, new routes. Generated overlay.

About these mocks

This session has no image generator, so the mocks are painted by a small renderer in this page: flat-shaded polygons, lamp falloff, haze, then a palette quantise with ordered dither to match the 32 texels-per-metre look. They show layout, light and module rhythm, not final art. Each plate's prompt is written for an image model; it states the room, the camera and the modules, and excludes modern elements. Reference images in design/reference images/ are cited by filename and not reproduced here.
