# Approval brief: the Garden Court (R07) and the Fungus Terraces (R15), phase 13

[Back to the front page](../../ENVIRONMENT_PLAN.md) · Status: **approved 2026-11-04 with the recommended answers** ·
Room types: [R07](../../design/room-atlas.html#R07) and [R15](../../design/room-atlas.html#R15) in the room atlas ·
How a room gets its feel: [room character](room-character.md)

## What this does, in one paragraph

Two new rooms, both about growing things, built one after the other. The
**Garden Court** is a four-part Persian garden under a big daylight shaft:
a pool, water channels, trees and beds, two storeys of arcades and a grand
iwan at the end. It is the brightest place in a stratum, the landmark you
steer by. The **Fungus Terraces** are stepped growing beds climbing a cavern
slope, lit only by the violet crop, with a cascade down the middle and
workers tending the beds. The garden is built by hand with sockets; the
terraces are generated to fit the slope. Each grows in the areas like the
cistern and the Bridge Hall did.

## The rooms' characters (proposed; agree them before building)

Both start from the atlas briefs. The changes are marked.

### R07 Garden Court

| Line | Proposed |
|---|---|
| **Feel** | Bright, calm, alive: the brightest place in a stratum |
| **Tint** | **Warm daylight white with blossom pink** (change: warmer than the atlas). The light-well is green-gold pulled to white and the Gorge is cool daylight, so the court goes warm and soft to stand apart from both |
| **Light** | One broad daylight shaft over the whole court, from a wide opening high above (Q3). It fills the court, not a narrow beam like the light-well. No lanterns by day. The arcades stay in cool shade, so the court is bright and its edges are pools of shadow. The doorway ends stay dark |
| **Landmark** | The four-part garden (chahar bagh) round its pool, closed by a grand iwan |
| **Motion** | Running water in the channels, the fountain, petals and moths drifting in the shaft |
| **Life** | Fruit trees and herb beds to forage (some edible, as in the Bridge Hall). Benches and a takht in the shade of the arcades. A gardener's traces: a basket, a pruning knife, a watering jar. Shelves in some of the arcade recesses |
| **Story** | Tended or overgrown, chosen per area (Q5): the Forgotten Wing's garden has gone wild, with vines over the arcades, a cracked channel and a dry fountain |

### R15 Fungus Terraces

| Line | Proposed |
|---|---|
| **Feel** | Fertile, strange, humming with work |
| **Tint** | **Violet** (as in the atlas), from the glowing caps. No room has it yet |
| **Light** | The crop only: violet caps on every terrace, brightest at the bed edges. Workers carry no flames (spores). The cascade catches the glow. The cavern roof stays dark |
| **Landmark** | The water cascade down the middle of the terraces, with the stair beside it |

| **Life** | Workers tending and harvesting (figures, as the Hypostyle scholars). Baskets, trays and tools at the bed ends. Mushrooms to pick. A shelter at the foot with sacks and a drying rack |
| **Story** | Tended terraces near the stair and the doors, wild and overgrown ones further up and out along the slope (Q8) |

### Neighbours

The areas put these rooms next to the Stack Galleries (blue), the Hypostyle
Stacks (red), the caravanserai (warm firelight), the cistern (teal), the
light-well (green-gold) and the Bridge Hall (silver mist). Both new colours
differ from all of them. Two pairings to avoid, which the area rules will
enforce: the Garden Court never opens straight off the light-well (two
daylight rooms in a row), and the Fungus Terraces never open straight off
the cistern (two rooms lit by plants in a row).

## What you'd get

- **The Garden Court** (R07): about 24 × 32 m (6 × 8 bays, as in the atlas),
  open to a light shaft above. Arcades on two storeys down both long sides;
  you can walk the lower arcade, the upper one is a gallery you see but don't
  reach in this version. A pool in the middle, four channels that split the
  garden into four beds, trees in rows, a grand iwan at the end. Doors at the
  near end and through the iwan. Sockets change the fountain (round pool,
  stepped cascade or low basin) and the planting (cypress, fruit trees or
  glowing lumen fungus for strata with no daylight).
- **The Fungus Terraces** (R15): 6 to 12 terraces, each a 2.8 m bed behind a
  1.5 m retaining wall with drain arches, climbing a cavern slope. A stair
  runs up beside the cascade, so the room joins two storeys: a door at the
  foot and one at the top (Q6). The slope fits the cavern the grammar is
  given, like the Bridge Hall's bridge.
- **Things to pick:** fruit and herbs in the garden, mushrooms on the
  terraces, through the same pickables as the Bridge Hall.
- **On the map:** the garden drawn as its four beds and pool; the terraces as
  their stair and steps.
- **Built in walked parts,** as the character guide asks: structure and
  landmark, then light, then mood, then life and story. Captures at each
  part; you walk the ones that matter. Route and frame tests once the look is
  final.

## The steps

1. **The Garden Court, its structure and light.** Kit pieces (two-storey
   arcade bay, grand iwan, channel, pool and fountain, beds, trees, shaft
   cap), the hand-built room with its sockets, and the daylight shaft. Placed
   in a test area so you can walk it.
2. **The Garden Court's life and story.** Foraging, benches, gardener's
   traces, petals and water, the overgrown state. Then it grows in the areas
   (Q2), with route audit, frame gate, save and resume, and the map.
3. **The Fungus Terraces, structure and light.** Terrace kit (bed, retaining
   wall, drain arch, cascade channel, stair flight), the terrace grammar that
   fits a slope, the violet crop glow. In a test area to walk.
4. **The Fungus Terraces' life and story.** Workers, tools, picking, spores,
   tended-to-wild gradient. Then it grows in the areas (Q2), with the same
   tests as step 2.

Each room is committed after its own walks, so R07 is in the game before R15
starts.

## Risks

- **Daylight in the garden and the light budget.** A bright court with one
  shadowed sun and fills must stay within 50 lights and 4 shadows, and the
  sky opening must not make the rooms beside it look dark when you look back
  through the door. Captures will check this first.
- **A big hand-built room in generated areas.** The caravanserai and Bridge
  Hall fit; a 6 × 8 bay court needs space, so it may grow in fewer seeds
  than the Bridge Hall's 11 of 20. A fit sweep reports how many.
- **Terraces on a slope.** The grammar must keep each terrace's stair, beds
  and drain arches clear of the route and the cascade; the route audit walks
  every terrace with the real player capsule.
- **Glow everywhere.** Many emissive caps can flatten the room. Keep pools of
  violet with dark between beds, as the light rules ask.

## Not included

- The garden changing with the world clock and seasons (sockets ready, the
  clock later).
- A walkable upper arcade (Q4).
- Farming as a game system (planting, growing, harvests over time); this
  phase gives picking only.
- Workers that move or talk.
