## What this does, in one paragraph

The gorge today is built one 4 m bay at a time: every bay picks its own
window, stack, tile panel or balcony, so nothing reads as a building, tile
lands anywhere, and decks have no way up. Phase 8 rebuilds it as a Persian
hill town around a few great buildings (direction C on the board): plain
brick and plaster houses stepping up both cliffs, rooms and paths cut into
the rock between them, tile only on portals, crown bands, domes and
minarets, pointed arches set deep in the walls, three or four great
waterfalls instead of many thin ones, and gardens that follow the light
(green under a shaft of natural light or a lumen stone, glowing fungi in the
dark). It starts with **bank 1**, the bank you see first from the Overlook,
and the camp's own front is brought into the same look.

## Built to be reused

The new pieces are a general Persian kit, not gorge-only pieces: house
blocks, openings (arcade bay, lattice window, coloured-glass window, house
door, portal), the lumen stone, fungi, cypress and garden beds are separate
kit pieces and prop scenes with their own materials, so any room or area can
use them. The gorge only adds the rules that arrange them.

## The steps

| Step | What you can try |
|---|---|
| 1. The Persian kit | A gallery scene (`rooms/test/persian_gallery.tscn`, pictures in `previews/p8_step1/`) with every new piece and plant lit as in the gorge: houses, the five openings, the portal, carved-room fronts, lumen stone, fungi, cypress, beds, pool and fountain, the great fall |
| 2. Bank 1 in the new look | Walk the Gorge: bank 1 is houses and carved rooms on terraces with stairs, the iwan garden lit by its lumen stone, fungal terraces in the dark, a shaft garden, one great fall. The other banks stay as they are for now |
| 3. The camp's front | The camp's facade, arch, pishtaq and terrace facing the gorge rebuilt with the new pieces and rules |
| 4. Budget check | Triangles and frame time for the Overlook view measured against the limits; the middle-distance buildings merged if needed |

After bank 1 is approved, the other banks follow bank by bank (later steps,
added here when bank 1 is settled).

## Decisions (your answers)

| | Question | Answer | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Direction | **C, monuments in a town** | About 85% houses and carved rooms, 15% landmark per view |
| Q2 | Tile | **Four places only**: portal, crown frieze, domes, minaret bands; revisited after the slice | No tiled wall fields or random tile panels |
| Q3 | Waterfalls | **Three or four great falls** | Bank 1 gets one; each pours from a channel mouth or spillway |
| Q4 | Life | **Dressing now, figures later** | Carpets, pots, awnings, windcatchers as kit dressing; people and caravans in a later phase |
| Q5 | Carved | **About a third of each bank**, more near the roof | Carved rooms, rock-cut paths and the iwan |
| Q6 | Gardens in the slice | **Yes**: the lumen-stone iwan garden, fungal terraces, one shaft garden; teal fungi, a second glow colour only if teal gets monotonous | Light decides what grows |
| Q7 | Where to start | **Bank 1 and one great fall**, then the rest | Compared with today from the same spots |

## Not included

- Banks 2–8, the sanctum and the far aqueducts (after bank 1 is settled).
- People, caravans and animals.
- The navigation map (now phase 9).
