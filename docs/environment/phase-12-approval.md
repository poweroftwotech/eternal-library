# Approval brief: the Bridge Hall (R21), phase 12

[Back to the front page](../../ENVIRONMENT_PLAN.md) · Status: **approved 2026-11-04 with the recommended answers, plus your two additions: natural plants, edible ones among them, and a dark cave passage at each door**  ·
Room type: [R21 in the room atlas](../../design/room-atlas.html#R21) (your idea, 2026-10-31) ·
How a room gets its feel: [room character](room-character.md)

## What this does, in one paragraph

The Bridge Hall is a piece of the gorge sized as a room, so an area can grow
one like any other room. You come through a door high in the wall of a tall
cavern hall. A straight stone bridge runs from your door across a drop into
mist to a door in the far wall. The side walls are carved into storeys of
Persian facades with some windows lit. Off to one side another, older bridge
crosses at a different height. You can see it but you can't reach it. This
phase builds the room, puts it in the Lower Stacks and the Forgotten Wing,
and checks it on 20 seeds. The first version has two doors. Four doors wait
until areas can have loops (see "Not included").

## The room's character (proposed; agree it before building)

The atlas's brief, with one change: the colour (Q5). The rooms an area puts
next to the Bridge Hall are the Stack Galleries (blue), the Hypostyle Stacks
(red) and the Cistern (teal). Cold blue-grey would read as more of the
Galleries.

| Line | Proposed |
|---|---|
| **Feel** | Vertiginous, echoing, awed: the biggest space indoors |
| **Tint** | **Silver-white mist with warm windows** (Q5): a pale, colourless haze from below, not blue. The lit windows in the walls give the only warm colour |
| **Light** | Each door opens off a short **dark cave passage** (your addition), so the light changes slowly between this room and the next. The mist glows from below, lit by a pale pool where the falls land far down (you see it through gaps in the mist). Lamps only at the two doors' ends of the bridge and on a few newels. Lit windows in the facades. No ceiling lights: the cavern roof stays dark |
| **Landmark** | The bridges side by side: yours, and the older one at another height |
| **Motion** | Mist rising and drifting under the decks; a waterfall pouring through an arch of the older bridge; echoing drips |
| **Life** | **Hanging gardens** (your addition): vines, figs and grapes trailing from the lit balconies and windows, herbs in the window boxes, mushrooms on the damp ledges in the dark, ferns and moss where the mist wets the rock. Some can be picked, as in the other rooms. A landing halfway across with a bench, a lamp and a keeper's traces (a basket, a book left open). From there you look across at the other bridge |
| **Story** | Your bridge is newer and plain; the older one is weathered, patched with timber where its parapet fell, and ends at a sealed door |

The doorway ends stay dark, as the light rules ask, so the gallery's blue
shows through behind you and the far door's lamp draws you across.

## What you'd get

- **A new room in the Lower Stacks and the Forgotten Wing,** at most one per
  area (Q1), grown off a gallery or the hall like the cistern and the
  light-well.
- **The cave passages** (your addition): each door opens onto a rough, unlit cave passage two bays long, with a bend where the space allows, before the hall opens out. Your eyes adjust in the dark as the light changes from one room to the next, and the bend hides each room's lamps from the other, so fewer lights are in reach at once. It is dim, never black: the next room's light shows round the bend, and a little glowing moss marks the floor.
- **Plants by light:** gardens hang where there is light (under the lit windows, on the balconies, round the bridge-end lamps); in the dark, mushrooms, moss and ferns, wet from the mist. Some are edible and can be picked (figs, grapes, herbs, mushrooms).
- **The hall:** a cavern from 5 bays wide and 6–8 bays long (20 × 24–32 m;
  Q2). Its two long walls are carved facades on several storeys: arcades,
  balconies, lit and dark windows, from the Persian kit of phase 8. Its roof
  is cave rock with stalactites. Below the decks is mist.
- **The bridge:** straight from above, door to door. It may climb or drop one
  storey on a flight set into its deck (Q3). The span's flight piece already
  does this, so the far door can open onto a different storey of the area.
- **The other bridge** (Q4): one older bridge at another height, parallel to
  yours, with a waterfall through one arch. Both its ends are sealed ornate
  doors (the ones from door travel). It is scenery for now, and a promise of
  the four-door version.
- **Safe to cross:** parapets with guards on every open side, as on the
  spans. If you get over one in fly mode or by a bug and fall, you're put back
  at the door you came in by (Q6).
- **On the map** (phase 11): the hall is drawn as its bridge and doors.
  The mist isn't drawn as floor.
- **On the Strata Board:** R21 shows up as a room type you can add to any
  area, with its own entry in the 20-seed sweep.

## Decisions (your answers: as recommended, 2026-11-04)

| | Question | Recommended | Why / what it means |

| Q1 | Where does it appear? | **The Lower Stacks and the Forgotten Wing, at most one per area, grown off the hall or a gallery** | These are the cave-side areas: the room says "the library is carved into rock". The Library Wing and the Sanctum are the kept, scholarly side. You can add it anywhere later on the Strata Board |
| Q2 | How big? | **5 bays wide, 6–8 bays long (24–32 m), mist 2 storeys down, roof 2 storeys up** | The atlas allowed spans to 40 m. A room this tall reserves every cell it covers on every storey, so a bigger hall would often fail to fit and the area would grow without it. The sweep in step 4 will tell us how often it fits; we can grow it if it fits easily |
| Q3 | Can the bridge change storey? | **Yes: level, one storey up or one storey down, a third each** | It's what makes the far door lead somewhere new in the area's height. The flight is the span's existing in-line flight, so it stays straight from above. Two storeys would need a 6-bay flight run and a longer hall |
| Q4 | The second bridge, without a door to reach it? | **Yes, as scenery, an older bridge with sealed doors at both ends** | The landmark is "bridges side by side": with only your bridge, the room is a corridor over a hole. Sealed doors say where the four-door version will go |
| Q5 | The room's colour | **Silver-white mist with warm windows** (changed from the atlas's cold blue-grey) | Its neighbours are the blue Stack Galleries, the teal Cistern and the red Hypostyle Stacks. Blue-grey would blur into the Galleries across the doorway. The other choice is to keep blue-grey and make the Galleries' doorway end warmer |
| Q6 | Falling off | **Back at the door you came in by** | The area's fall line is far below the hall's mist, so today you'd fall a long way and land at the area's start. Putting you back at the room's door is kinder and is what the Gorge should do too |

## Risks

- **Fitting it in.** The hall is about 5 × 8 cells on 5 storeys, far bigger
  than any room so far except the hypostyle hall. If areas often can't place
  it, I'll show you the sweep's numbers and we pick: a smaller hall, or a
  recipe rule that grows it early, before the galleries take the space.
- **The cave passages make the room bigger.** Two bays at each door, plus the bend, add to the space the planner has to find (the fitting risk above).
- **Light budget.** The cave passages keep the neighbours' lights out of sight. Inside, you see the whole hall at once, so the bridge-end lamps,
  window lights and the neighbours' lights all count toward the 50 in reach.
  Lit windows will be glowing glass without a light of their own, except a
  few near the bridge. The mist glow is one wide light below the decks.
- **Triangles.** Facade walls on 5 storeys both sides are a lot of geometry.
  They will be batched (the kit batcher), and the far storeys use plainer
  facade bays. The frame gate in step 4 decides.
- **The map.** The map draws rooms from their cells. The Bridge Hall's
  walkable cells are only the deck, so it will be drawn as a bridge between
  two doors with the hall's outline faint around it. I'll show you a capture
  of the open map.
- **Seen through the doorway.** From a gallery the hall's mist and windows
  show through the door. That's the "next room's light announces it" rule,
  but the planner's sight rule (rooms you can see into from a door) has to
  treat the open hall as fully seen, as it does the light-well.

## Not included

- **Four doors and two walkable bridges.** Areas are trees: one way into each
  room. A second walkable bridge, and every room past its doors, could not be
  reached. That waits for loops in the area layout, which is its own phase.
- **Three doors** (a bridge that branches on a landing).
- **Bridges at angles.** All bridges run end wall to end wall, straight.
- **Anything that happens on the bridge** (wind, creatures, a bridge that
  breaks). Spooky is fine; danger isn't, as for every room so far.
